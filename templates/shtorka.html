01 / Collect. Prepare. Share.

From working files
to ready to share.

For independent developers, designers and people who make digital products. Keep the handoff close to the work.

  1. 01 / Collect

    A shelf at the top.

    Drag files or folders to the top edge of your Mac. Select what you need, use Quick Look, or reveal the original in Finder. Your shelf stays with you after restarting the app.

  2. 02 / Prepare

    Make it ready.

    Create a ZIP or a separate copy in a supported format. Keep a set of working files together while preparing the result you want to pass on.

  3. 03 / Share

    Pass the work on.

    Send the selected set to Finder, another app or through AirDrop. Keep the collection close while you finish the handoff.

These workflows are present in the development build. Conversion support is limited to the validated format matrix; the supported list will be published with the beta.

02 / Your day

Your day.
Your layout.

Choose three blocks and adjust their sizes. A day or week calendar, music, Shortcuts, notes, tasks or a timer — around the way you work.

The panel opens on hover and stays tucked away when idle. The date stays visible in the calendar even on a day without events.

Music information and controls depend on the supported source.

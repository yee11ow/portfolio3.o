Where I fit, and where I don't

I don't work in WordPress, Webflow or Shopify. Rather than find that out on a call, here is the split.

If your build lives in a CMS: take the design layer. You get a structured Figma file your developers build straight from: components, states, responsive behaviour, redlines where they matter.

If it's a standalone site: I take the whole thing. Design, hand-coded front end, accessibility, launch on your client's domain. Static delivery means fast loads, no plugin surface and nothing to patch every month.

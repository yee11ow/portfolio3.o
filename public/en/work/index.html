Audit & accounting Uppsala · Live concept

Audit firm: from credibility to a clear enquiry route

  1. The problem

    The original review recorded a fixed-width mobile layout, conflicting history statements and a phone number that was not a link. These observations became requirements for a clearer route to contact.

  2. What I did

    I mapped the journey from understanding a service to assessing the team and choosing contact. The case study shows provisional user profiles, retrospective wireframes and the responsive homepage concept.

  3. What exists

    A working front-end concept, with a plan to test findability and contact completion. No client launch or increase in enquiries is claimed.

Law firm Uppsala & Sala · Live concept

Law firm: task-based routes through a bankruptcy

  1. The problem

    People seeking legal advice and people dealing with a bankruptcy have different tasks. The review also recorded a zoom restriction and non-functional share controls.

  2. What I did

    I designed separate routes for creditors, former employees and landlords or buyers, with case selection and structured information. The study shows the user flows, wireframes and actual prototype screens.

  3. What exists

    An independent interface demonstration. Real receiving and case routing are future implementation work; usability sessions and operational measurement are planned, not completed.

Audit & accounting Uppsala · Live concept

FB Revision: fifty years of credentials behind a 2014 website

  1. The problem

    Fourteen staff, three authorised auditors and roots back to the 1950s. None of it was visible. The homepage had no H1 at all, every page was tagged lang="en-US" despite Swedish content, the phone number could not be tapped on a mobile, and the start page celebrated fifty years while the services page said thirty.

  2. What I did

    The content was good. It was the shell around it that was from 2014. I kept the substance and rebuilt the frame: one timeline that settles the contradiction, the authorisations moved directly under the headline, a phone number that dials on one tap, and mobile first throughout. Plus a print stylesheet, because accountants really do print things.

  3. The detail

    The concept carries its own audit: nine findings from the live site, each paired with its fix. Everything from the four GIFs on the homepage to the WordPress edit stamp reading “admin 2018-11-29” on the staff page.

Law firm Uppsala & Sala · Live concept

Nordström Advokater: a bankruptcy portal for the people it hits

  1. The problem

    Six of the firm’s eight lawyers are appointed bankruptcy trustees by Uppsala District Court. Every bankruptcy leaves suppliers, dismissed employees and landlords hunting for the right person. The site offered them one general contact form. It also shipped user-scalable=0, so a visitor could not pinch to enlarge the text.

  2. What I did

    I turned the firm’s strongest asset into a portal of its own. The visitor picks their situation, say a claim, unpaid wages or a lease, and lands straight with the trustee who holds the case, with the case number and where it stands. The zoom lock I removed. One line of code, and still the most important change in the whole concept.

  3. The care

    The whole concept is noindex. Someone searching a company’s bankruptcy must never land in a design study and file a real claim into a form that goes nowhere.

How I work

How it works, step by step.

  1. 01

    Intro call

    Fifteen free minutes about the firm, your clients and what is not working today. No sales pitch.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Within a week you get a clickable concept of your start page. If the direction feels wrong, you pay nothing.

  3. 03

    Build and launch

    I build the whole site, launch it on your domain and hand over a short guide for updates. Two weeks of adjustments are included.

  • Design file

Terms

You pay nothing until you have seen and approved the concept. Only then is 50% invoiced and the build starts, with the balance on delivery. Invoicing runs through Frilans Finans (egenanställning), so you get an ordinary invoice with VAT from a Swedish company. Your finance department has no F-tax question to resolve.

After launch

The site is yours and you can look after it yourself, the handover guide covers what changes most often. If you would rather not, ongoing care is available as a monthly subscription: updates, text changes, new pages when you need them and an eye kept on that everything works. From 900 kr a month, agreed separately and never a requirement.

How I work

How it works, step by step.

  1. 01

    Intro call

    Twenty free minutes about the firm, your clients and what is not working today. No sales pitch.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Once the scope is approved and the first instalment is paid, you receive a clickable homepage concept within one week. One consolidated revision round is included before the direction is locked.

  3. 03

    Build and launch

    I build the whole site, launch it on your domain and hand over a short guide for updates. Two weeks of adjustments are included.

Included
  • Design file
  • Responsive build
  • Copy structure
  • SEO basics
  • Accessibility (WCAG)
  • Launch on your domain
  • 2 weeks of adjustments
Terms

After the intro call, you receive a written scope and fixed price. Once approved, 50% is invoiced to reserve the project and begin the concept work. The remaining 50% is invoiced before launch and handover. Invoicing runs through Frilans Finans (egenanställning), so you get an ordinary invoice with VAT from a Swedish company. Your finance department has no F-tax question to resolve.

After launch

The site is yours and you can look after it yourself; the handover guide covers what changes most often. If you would rather not, ongoing care is available as a separate arrangement for updates, copy changes and technical oversight. It is priced by scope and required response time and is never a requirement.

2. Personal data

This site has no forms, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Simply reading it does not hand over any personal data to me.

If you email me, I process your address and whatever you write, in order to answer you and to discuss possible work. The legal basis is steps taken at your request prior to a contract, and my legitimate interest in replying (GDPR Art. 6.1 b and f).

How long: I keep the correspondence while the matter is live and then for twelve months from our last contact, after which it is deleted. If you would like it deleted sooner, just say so.

If you book a call, the booking is handled by Cal.com, which receives the name, email address and time you enter. That happens on their site, under their terms, and only after you follow the link.

Hosting. The site runs on Cloudflare Pages (Cloudflare, Inc.), which records ordinary technical connection data such as IP address, browser and time in order to serve and protect the site. Cloudflare acts as a processor under its data processing addendum, and transfers outside the EU/EEA are covered by the standard contractual clauses in that addendum.

3. Cookies and browser storage

This site sets no cookies.

The site stores nothing in your browser either. The language is decided by the address: Swedish lives at / and English at /en/. There is no preference to remember and no identifier to set.

The one exception is the AI assistant: if you start it, ElevenLabs may store values of its own. That is described in section 4.

4. The AI assistant, Alex

Alex is an AI, not a person. He says so in his first sentence, and confirms it plainly if you ask.

None of the assistant loads when you open the page. Only when you press Start the assistant is the code fetched and a connection opened. That press is your consent, and it is also the legal basis (GDPR Art. 6.1 a). If you do not press it, nothing happens.

Who processes what. The assistant is run by ElevenLabs, Inc. in the US, and the language model behind the answers is Google Gemini. What is processed is whatever you say or type, a voice recording if you speak, and anything you choose to give, such as your name, email address and which firm you are from.

How long. Conversations and recordings are kept for twelve months and then deleted. If you want that done sooner, just email me.

What the assistant does not do. It makes no decisions about you, does not profile you, and cannot enter into agreements. It answers questions and notes a time. Price, contract and scope are mine to decide.

If you would rather not. Email hello@matviiakkuratov.com or book a time directly. Then no AI is involved and no data leaves this domain.

Once the assistant has started, ElevenLabs may store values of its own in your browser to hold the conversation together. That is outside my control and is described in their own policy.

5. Third parties

Fonts are served from this domain, not from a font CDN. There is no analytics, no tag manager, no tracking pixel and no embedded social media. Loading a page on this site does not disclose your IP address to any third party.

There are two exceptions, and both require you to act. Starting the AI assistant fetches it from ElevenLabs, as described in section 4. Following a link onward to Cal.com, LinkedIn or one of the concept sites means the other party’s own policy applies from there. Data leaves the page only when you choose to make it.

6. Your rights

You may request access to the data I hold about you, have it corrected or erased, object to or restrict the processing, and ask for a copy in a portable format (GDPR Art. 15–21). Email me and I will deal with it.

If you are unhappy with how I handle it, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, imy.se.

7. The design concepts

The two concepts shown here, for Företagarnas Revisionsbyrå i Uppsala AB (FB Revision) and Nordström Advokater AB, are self-initiated proposals. Neither firm ordered them, paid for them, reviewed them or approved them, and nothing here should be read as a client relationship, a recommendation or an endorsement.

Company names, trade names and any text quoted from the firms’ own pages remain the property of the respective company, and appear here only to present the proposals. The concept sites themselves are marked as concepts and excluded from search engines, so that nobody looking for the actual firm ends up in a design study.

If either firm would prefer their concept not to be shown, email me and I will take it down. No discussion needed.

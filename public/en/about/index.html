About

I work with firms whose most valuable asset is judgement.

I’m Matvii, and I work with firms across Sweden in law, audit, accounting and consulting. In those fields the website has to carry your credibility before anyone has met you.

My background is in fashion and photography. That is where the eye for restraint comes from: fewer elements, better typography, and nothing on the page that is not doing work.

I design in Figma and build sites that load fast, work for everyone, and hold up on a phone between two meetings.

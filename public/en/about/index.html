About

I work with firms whose most valuable asset is judgement.

I’m Matvii, a UI/UX and web designer based in Stockholm. I work on websites for firms in law, audit, accounting and consulting, where clarity and trust matter before the first conversation.

My background is in B2B sales and marketing. Working with decision-makers and turning business needs into scoped proposals taught me to connect design choices to a specific problem and a useful next step.

My work spans website reviews, requirements, information architecture, user flows, wireframes and responsive UI. I design in Figma and implement selected projects in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The case studies distinguish what I built from what still needs user validation.

01 / Collect. Prepare. Share.

From working files
to ready to share.

For independent developers, designers and people who make digital products. Keep the handoff close to the work.

  1. 01 / Collect

    A shelf at the top.

    Drag files or folders to the top edge of your Mac. Select what you need, use Quick Look, or reveal the original in Finder. Your shelf stays with you after restarting the app.

  2. 02 / Prepare

    Make it ready.

    Create a ZIP or a separate copy in a supported format. Keep a set of working files together while preparing the result you want to pass on.

  3. 03 / Share

    Pass the work on.

    Send the selected set to Finder, another app or through AirDrop. Keep the collection close while you finish the handoff.

These workflows are present in the development build. Conversion support is limited to the validated format matrix; the supported list will be published with the beta.

03 / Before you join

A few useful details.

Compatibility, formats and permissions will be documented before distribution. The app is being prepared for its first external beta.

Can I download SHTORKA now?+

The closed beta is being prepared. SHTORKA is not available as a public download yet. Use Join the beta to request an invitation by email; release dates have not been announced.

How much will it cost?+

Price, purchase model and trial terms will be confirmed before sales begin. Requesting a beta invitation does not involve a payment.

Will it work on my Mac?+

SHTORKA is being developed for macOS. Supported macOS versions, processors and required permissions will be documented before beta distribution.

Which formats and music sources work?+

ZIP creation and supported-format copies are part of the development build. Conversion support follows a validated matrix. Music controls depend on the supported source. Exact format and source lists will be published with the beta.

What happens after I join?+

Your email goes directly to Matvii, the maker of SHTORKA. Sending it registers your interest in the beta. An invitation and access to the app will be arranged separately once the beta is ready.

Where can I read about privacy?+

Website privacy explains browsing and beta email enquiries. The app’s permissions and data policy will be documented before it is distributed.

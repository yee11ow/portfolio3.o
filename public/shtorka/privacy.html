SHTORKA / Website privacy

Privacy, in plain language.

This website introduces SHTORKA, a compact file shelf for Mac in development by Matvii Akkuratov. Questions about your data can be sent to hello@matviiakkuratov.com.

Browsing the site

This site does not use analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, localStorage or sessionStorage. Page controls operate in your browser. Copying the email address uses your clipboard only when you click Copy email. Fonts and other assets are served with the website.

The hosting provider processes technical connection information, including your IP address, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Asking about the beta

The beta link opens your email application. Nothing is submitted until you send the email yourself. If you write, Matvii receives the email address and information you choose to include, uses it to respond to your enquiry and records your interest in SHTORKA.

Your message is kept while the enquiry is active and for up to 12 months afterward. You can withdraw your interest or request access, correction or deletion by writing to the address above. You may also contact your data protection authority; in Sweden, this is IMY.

The app

This page describes this website and email enquiries. The application’s permissions, compatibility and privacy information will be documented before it becomes available.

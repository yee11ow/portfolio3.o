01 / The idea

Less in the way.
More room to work.

Nova starts with the idea of a panel for developers on Mac: a useful layer that belongs in your workspace, without becoming the whole workspace.

This is the direction behind the project. The precise tools, interface and interactions are being developed and will be shown as they become ready.

02 / Good to know

A few things,
before you jump in.

Can I download Nova now?+

Nova is in development and is not available to download yet. You can request early access by email. A release date has not been announced.

How much will it cost?+

Pricing and the purchase model are still being decided. Requesting early access does not involve a payment or a purchase commitment.

Will it work on my Mac?+

Nova is being developed for macOS. The minimum macOS version and supported processors will be confirmed before a download is offered.

What happens after I get in touch?+

Your email goes directly to Matvii, the maker of Nova. He can reply about the project and your interest in testing it. This is a personal enquiry, not an automatic account or a confirmed beta invitation.
